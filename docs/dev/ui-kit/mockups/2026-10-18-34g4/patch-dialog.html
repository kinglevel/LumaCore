Patch setup dialog

Round 2 (2026-10-18, for 34g4 part 3). The dialog is Devices › DMX › Patch: the claim and the draft cover all of Devices (built in part 2: Claim Root.ShowData.Devices or enter patch). The left side is the MVR tree (decision point 12): the MVR layers and their nested groups from Devices.Metadata.MVR, where a fixture's place is one link, its MVR parent. A switch groups the sidebar by MVR tree, MVR class or tag; private sorting is tags. One <luma-grid> serves the Live Patch and the draft; its columns are the fixture's declared properties, and the live ones are the build's (Name, Note, Address, PosX…RotZ, MasterReact). This round is for Live, Draft, the sidebar, the ≡ menu, Duplicate, Metadata and Exit. The import and export screens (after the divider in the strip) are still round 1, drawn before point 12; they get their own round with parts 4–6.

Round 1 · parts 4–6:
One grid, three modes
  • The rows are fixtures by GUID, and the columns come from the fixture's declared properties (the 12d rule).
  • Live: a column is editable when its property is declared live (Name, Note, Address, PosX…RotZ, MasterReact). Your change reaches the rig at once as your own Oops step, and Bob's draft follows it, unless Bob changed that cell: then it is refused, naming him. Every other column is greyed with a lock.
  • Draft: every column is editable. Exit (bottom right) leaves the patch: Save / Discard / Cancel. Save is one Oops step for everyone.
  • Tags is a column like the others (the Tags cell from the Tags round 2); it is locked in the Live Patch.
  • Import: the same grid, with a Status column, cell marks and ticks. The slab's function block switches to the import's actions.
What the import marks mean
  • Orange with a tick: taken from the file (the dialog's switch was Take); the live value is under it.
  • Dashed: offered and not taken (the switch was Offer); the file's value is under it.
  • Green: a new fixture. Red row: LOST, kept unless Delete is ticked. Red cell: a clash, which is never taken and blocks Keep.
  • Blue GUID: the GUID was the match. Gold dot: Bob's own edit. Violet dot: someone's live edit that reached the draft.
The sidebar: the MVR tree
  • MVR layers at the top, their groups nested under them (MVR's GroupObjects), with folding arrows and guide lines. A fixture sits in one place: its MVR parent, a layer or a group. The selected node filters the grid and is where New puts fixtures.
  • A layer or group made here (Touring, Specials) is marked here: it gets a UUID of its own and goes out with an export. A re-import puts new fixtures in their group by UUID; a fixture the drawing moved shows as a change of its group.
  • The switch above the tree groups the same fixtures by MVR class (from the drawing; read only here) or by tag (your private sorting; Direct / Deep as in the Tags pool). Fixtures with no group, class or tag are listed last.
  • The selected group (or the one under the pointer) shows a ≡ menu: New group, Rename, Copy, Duplicate…, Move…, Select fixtures, Delete…. The bar under the tree has "+ Layer" and "+ Group". In the Live Patch they are greyed: the tree is part of the draft.
  • Duplicate asks for clean fixtures or cloned data (all, or chosen kinds: presets, groups, sequences, effects, layouts, masters) and a first FID (the next free block, spacing kept); the copies are unpatched, with new GUIDs and no MVR source. Delete deletes the group's fixtures too, after one question naming what uses them.
  • Dragging fixtures onto a group moves them (their MVR parent changes). Dragging a group onto another nests it.
Devices › DMX › Patch
  • The claim chip and Exit belong to Devices: one draft for every device kind, and Exit saves or discards all of it.
  • The kind rail lists the kinds under Devices: DMX now (Devices.Dmx), Metadata (MVR layers and classes, shared with 3DSpatial), and a dashed slot for kinds that come later.
  • Inside DMX the strip has Patch, Fixture types and Universes, the parts of Devices.Dmx.
Import, then back to the draft
  • Under review nothing is in the draft yet. The ticks come from the dialog's Take / Offer switches, and you change them per cell.
  • The gold button reads "Import ticked (22)". New fixtures land in their MVR group by UUID; only when that group was deleted here does it ask where they go (one pop-up per such group: ★ make the group again, pick a group, or leave them in No MVR group; "Use for the other deleted groups"). Then it writes the ticked changes into the draft and returns to it. Cancel import leaves the draft as it was.
  • Scope: both sides are filtered, but a GUID is matched across the whole patch. A fixture matched outside the scope shows as OUTSIDE with "Bring into House", and only fixtures inside the scope can be LOST.
  • After the import, the rows it changed keep a thin edge mark until Exit, so you can see what came from the file.
