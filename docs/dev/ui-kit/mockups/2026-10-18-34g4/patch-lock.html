Patch: one tile, a claim button

Mockup for the 34g4 patch entry hardening (decisions §34, 2026-10-18). The dialog always opens Live. The bottom-right button reads Live: hold it to claim and edit, and it reads Claimed · Draft; press it to go back to Live. Pick a state, then try the hold and the press.

Live
Live patch. Only the live columns can be edited (Name, Note, Address, position, MasterReact); the rest are locked. A hold runs Claim Root.ShowData.Devices; a press only says "Hold to claim and edit".
Claimed · yours
Gold: you edit the draft and everyone else is locked out. A press runs Unclaim Root.ShowData.Devices: Save / Discard / Cancel when the draft changed, back to Live at once when not.
Claimed · Bob
Greyed and dashed. A hold does nothing; only Bob ends his draft. An Admin can still use /Force on the command line.

The ✕ while you have it claimed. There is no "keep claimed": a draft never outlives its dialog. With nothing changed the ✕ goes back to Live and closes without asking.

4 · The holder is lost

Logout, disconnect or a lost station: the claim is released and the draft is discarded, nothing kept. Everyone's button turns back to Live; the System Monitor says so.
