What to try
  • Tap a tile to select it; drag the tile or the selection. A gold line shows where it lands, a drop on a column header puts it at the bottom, and a lasso on empty space selects several.
  • Hold a tile to open its details (compare the three presentations): what the GDTF says, why it was placed there, which fixture types use it, its channel functions, and this profile's readout and resolution.
  • With New, the old attributes fold into "+n existing ▾"; with All everything shows. Page lines every 4 tiles match the encoder pages.
  • The chips filter, and the search box highlights matches across the columns.
  • Drop on + New group to make a group; it asks for the name.
  • After a move, Apply to similar names? appears with an editable pattern.
  • On your profile, a small show: X mark shows where the show differs.
  • Phone: two columns at a time, a dot map on top, and auto-scroll while dragging.
