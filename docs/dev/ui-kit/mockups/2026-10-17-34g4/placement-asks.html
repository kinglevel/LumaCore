Where an import puts new and moved fixtures

The example: Bob imported R12 with Automatic, then sorted by hand. He moved 6 fixtures into his own layer "Specials" and renamed the layer made from group "Truss 2" to "Mid truss". R13 arrives with 4 new fixtures in group Truss 2, 6 new in a new group Truss 4, and 2 fixtures the drawing moved from Truss 1 to Truss 3. Automatic never moves anything without asking. Here are three ways to ask. ★ marks the suggestion.

1 · A Layers step, one row per group

The review gets a second strip tab, Layers. It has one row per MVR group with new or moved fixtures, each with a choice. "Import ticked" waits until every row is answered.

For
  • Every layer question is on one page: 3 rows, not 12 fixtures.
  • The suggestion is already selected, so "Take all suggestions" answers everything in one tap.
  • It fits the review: Fixtures for the values, Layers for where they go.
Against
  • One more tab to visit. You have to notice "1 to answer" (the lane and the button say it).
  • The answer is per group. One odd fixture means Pick… for its group, or fixing it in the tree afterwards.

2 · A pop-up per group at Import ticked

Nothing to visit. Pressing Import ticked asks each layer question in turn, as station dialogs, before anything is written.

Against
  • A big rig can ask 15 times in a row, and you can't see the whole picture at once.
  • A wrong answer means cancelling the import and starting over, or fixing it in the tree afterwards.

3 · A Layer cell on each fixture

Placement is just another column in the review grid. New and moved fixtures get a Layer offer with the suggestion filled in. Select many rows and set them in one go.
