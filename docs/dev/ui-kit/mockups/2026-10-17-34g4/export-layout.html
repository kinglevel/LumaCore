MVR export: by MVR layer or by Luma layer

The example show: Bob imported Festival_R12 (Vectorworks), then sorted by hand. He renamed the layer made from group Truss 2 to "Mid truss", moved 6 fixtures from Truss 1 into his own layer "Specials", and patched a Touring floor package in Luma (no CAD behind it). Export MVR offers two layouts, shown here as the exported file's tree. The names in brackets are where each part comes from.

Both layouts
  • Each fixture keeps its UUID (its GUID), MVR class, IDs, addresses, type and mode. The GDTFs are embedded.
  • Anything made only for the export (a layer or group with no CAD behind it) gets a UUID once. That UUID is kept in Metadata, so the next export reuses it and the receiving software sees the same objects.
  • What gets exported: the dialog's view (Bob's draft while he holds it, else the live patch), as the whole patch, a Luma layer, or the selected fixtures.
The choice
  • A switch in the Export MVR dialog: Layout By MVR layer | By Luma layer, saved with the export settings like the import's preset.
  • A suggestion: By MVR layer when sending back to CAD, By Luma layer for a visualiser or another desk.
