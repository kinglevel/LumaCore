The fifth problem of the phone audit. The DMX map draws each universe as one bar of 512 slots across the dialog's width (dmx-map.html v2, 4d2). On a phone that bar is ~260 px: a 14-slot fixture becomes a 13 px block — the FID reads "1(" — and the free runs are slivers you can't tap. Shown in decision 1's folded frame (title chip, List | Map in the band, the one-bar slab). Every frame is live: tap a block; in B tap a universe.

The problem — real capture

The numbers
Bar 260 px for 512 slots = 0.5 px a slot. A 14-slot fixture 7 px of content; a 48-slot one 24 px.
The map's job
See how full each universe is, where the conflicts are, which fixture sits where; tap a block to select, a free run to patch the selection there, Zoom in to Edit Patch.

The options — 390 × 844, universe 1 with 12 fixtures, universe 2 with an overlap

To decide: one of A–C. Recommended: A · wrap the bar — on a phone each universe's 512 slots wrap into four lanes of 128 (the `u:aaa` label of each lane's first slot above it), so a block is four times wider (a 14-slot fixture ~40 px, its FID readable) and stays tappable, and the map still shows every universe at once.
