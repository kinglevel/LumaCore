The third problem of the phone audit. Phones get the one-bar slab now (decisions 1 and 2), but on a tablet — and on any desktop dialog drawn narrow — the slab's cells shrink with the dialog and their labels run into each other: "Edit PatchNext free addres", "Insert fixtures.". The function block's labels are the long ones.

The problem — the patch slab, cropped from the captures

Where
Every slab with a function block (Patch; Network's cells "Networks…", "Settings tab", "Multicast base" too) below about 1000 px of dialog width.
Why
A cell's label never wraps and isn't clipped at its edge: a long label overflows into the next cell.

The options — drag the width

Recommended: B · short labels — each action may declare a short form ("Next free", "Insert…", "Edit Patch…") the slab uses when the cell is narrow; one without a short form wraps to two lines.
