The home mark on settings cells: keep, move or drop

The user, 2026-10-20: "remove the 'In the show · every station' string on all buttons in the whole UI". The mark was decided in §30 "12d" (the SetupSettings board: "every cell says so"): each settings cell carries its kind badge, its name and where the setting lives — In the show · every station or This station · never in the show (short forms in the show / this station below 760 px). This page is the discussion: where the words are today, what they are for, and three ways to lose the repetition without losing the fact.

Real capture — the Import MVR dialog's Options step today

Where it is drawn
Every <luma-settings> cell (settings.js homeMark), every edit-card cell (editcard.js), and once in a setup menu's title row for the tab (setup.js HOME_TEXT: "This station · never in the show", "In the show · every station", or a menu's own words such as "Wiring: this station's row in the show · identity: this station").
What it says
Which of the three homes a value has (CLAUDE.md "State ownership"): replicated show data, or station-local, never replicated. A real distinction on a rig with several stations — a wrong guess means a setting that doesn't follow you to the next station.
How often it differs
Of the declared properties 19 are station-local, the rest (the default) in the show; of the menus' tabs 11 are show, 7 station. A tab is nearly always uniform: its header already says it once. The exceptions: the Network menu (wiring in the show, identity per station) and a grid's edit card over mixed rows.
Tests and docs
kit-12d.spec.ts asserts the marks exist (two lines); decisions §30 "12d" and CLAUDE.md's Menus bullet name them.

The options — the same four cells (three show settings, one station setting)

To decide: A, B or C. Recommended: A · the words go from every cell; the tab header says it once; the badge's tooltip keeps the fact — the repetition is gone wherever it was, nothing is lost for a rig with several stations (hover or hold the kind badge, read the header), and no new element is needed. B keeps a per-cell signal only where it carries information; C keeps the status quo on the mixed tabs.
