Finding 3 · Capture's rigs have no fixture semantics; the planning tools split in two

A Capture export carries one stripped GDTF per type whose every channel is the attribute Dummy — no Dimmer, no Pan, no Colour. The patch comes in (addresses, positions, numbers) but the programmer has nothing to say to such a fixture. Vectorworks and Depence embed the manufacturer's file.
