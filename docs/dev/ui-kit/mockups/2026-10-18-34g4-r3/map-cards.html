4d4 · 2 · the phone map has nothing to drag

The second question of the 4d4 hardening. On the desk the Map is a bar per universe with the fixtures' blocks on it: tap a block selects, drag it along its universe or onto another one, tap a free run with fixtures selected patches them there (§34 round 3, 18). §37 made the phone's Map a list of cards — number, name, used of 512, how many fixtures, a colour strip — and a card is a door: a tap zooms into that universe's slot grid, where question 1's hold lifts a run. So a phone has no block to tap and no bar to drop on. How does a phone move a fixture to another universe: only inside the slot grid (question 3 then has to work on a phone), or do the cards take a drop — or the selection?

The question — real capture, what is fixed

Decided, the desk
The Map's bars: tap a block selects (Shift / Meow adds), a drag moves it along its universe or onto another one's bar (the ghost snaps, gold / red), tap a free run with fixtures selected patches them there — one Assign Fixture … At DMX u.aaa line. A tablet keeps the bars.
Decided, the phone (§37 5–6)
Universes › Map is a list of cards; a tap on a card zooms into that universe's slot grid (rows of 10, ‹ back to the list); the conflict list stays under the cards. Edit Patch on a phone is full screen: the current fixture on one line with ‹ ›, the universe bar with ‹ ›, the grid, the function bar.
Decided, question 1
In the grid the hold is the press: hold still on a run, it lifts (the run pops, a chip above the finger names it and where it lands), let go on a slot is the line; hold and let go without moving is the context sheet; a plain finger scrolls.
Found on the way
On a phone a card opens Edit Patch with no fixtures: the setup hands it the map's own selection (the blocks' taps), which a phone never has — the Patch list's selection is not carried. Whichever option wins, a card should open Edit Patch with the Patch list's selection (the selection chip already knows it).
