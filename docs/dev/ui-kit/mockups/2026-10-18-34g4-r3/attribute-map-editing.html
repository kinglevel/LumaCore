Attribute map · editing by hand

Mockup for the 34g4 round 3 hardening (decisions §34, 2026-10-18): two ways to edit a map row by hand, both working — try them with a mouse or a finger, then choose. The same element serves the change map, Defined attributes and Clone's map. Real pair: MAC Viper Profile · Basic → MAC Quantum Profile · Basic (both in library/gdtf/). Five Viper attributes have no same-name target: Gobo2Pos, Gobo3, the animation wheel, Frost1, Prism1Pos. Colour is one row, converted as a whole (decided).

A · Rows and a target palette

Each Viper attribute is a row; its targets are chips. Drag a palette item onto a row to add it; drag a chip to another row to move it; drag a chip back to the palette or tap its × to remove it (the row becomes "kept unused"). On touch, press and move. Every hand edit becomes a rule (gold), listed at the bottom.

B · Two columns joined by lines

Viper on the left, Quantum on the right, a line for each mapping (QLC+'s remapping). Drag from a gold dot to a Quantum attribute to add a line; tap a line to remove it. A left item with no line is kept unused. The same rules list at the bottom.
