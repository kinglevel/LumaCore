Sorting fixtures into the tree: select, then drag onto a node

Your redirection: the sidebar as it is (nodes only), the Live patch pane shows fixtures and never a folder or a group, and fixtures get into the tree by selecting them and dragging them onto the sidebar's nodes. The lab is reworked that way (v2, same link): Patch tree lab. Below, the suggestions that fall out of it, each a rule you can accept or change; the question at the end is the one that touches the words.

Suggestions

1 · The paneA flat list of fixtures, scoped by the sidebar's pick: Deep (the default) shows everything beneath the node, Direct only the fixtures that sit on it (a stage picked Direct shows nothing — its fixtures are in its folders; the count says so). "Patch · all" is every fixture; "No MVR node" the ones outside every node.
2 · Where a fixture isA new column MVR node: the node the fixture sits in (its name; the full path on hover), sortable — sorted by it the flat list falls into its folders without any folder row. The default order stays the tree's (a node's fixtures together, in their order), by number / name / address when that header is tapped.
3 · The dragSelect rows (tap, Thru, ± , lasso) and lift them onto a sidebar node; the node lights gold under the pointer, a chip follows the pointer ("6 fixtures → Smoke"), the drop runs one line: Assign Fixture 1216 Thru 1221 At Fixture Media 9 — the shape Assign … At MvrGroup 2 has today, widened to a folder fixture (E-PATCH-011's list grows by one kind). One Oops step. Dropping on the layer row puts them on the layer itself; "No MVR node" is not a drop target (a fixture leaves the tree only by Delete).
4 · Moving a nodeDrag a sidebar node onto another: the one nesting ask ("Fixture Media 7003 "ACME Icarus" becomes a child of Fixture Media 2 "Stage 2 Keylights" (286 fixtures)?"), then Move Fixture Media 7003 At Fixture Media 2 /NoConfirmation. At a node nests, At a number renumbers; a folder At a number is refused.
5 · Without a pointer to drag withThe context menu of selected rows: Move into… picks the node from a list (the lab has it); on a phone, where the tree is a sheet, that is the only way. The line is the same.
6 · New fixturesInsert fixtures… and Store Fixture land under the picked node: the dialog runs the store, then Assign Fixture 30001 Thru 30004 At Fixture Media 1 as the second statement of the same line — no new flag, no new syntax. With "all" picked they land in no node, as today.
7 · The node's own menuIn the sidebar, as today for a group: Move…, Rename…, New folder here (a Grouping-type fixture under it, its next free Generic id), Delete — the Delete question is the one below.
8 · Counts and wordsSidebar counts are fixtures only ("1 348 fixtures · 23 folders"); ls, the tree chip and the list bar follow. List Fixture Media 1 lists the node's fixtures (Deep), Info Fixture Media 1 works, Delete Fixture Media 7006 /NoConfirmation finds its fixture — the three captures on page 1 fixed.
