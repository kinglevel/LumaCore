Question 1 · what does the sidebar's tree show?

A · Recommended

One tree: layers › groups › folder fixtures, nested as the show has them

A fixture that holds fixtures is a node of the MVR tree, drawn under its parent (a layer, a group or another folder) with its name, its second id (Media 1) and its count, folded like the layer today. Picking it filters the grid to what is under it; the Tag grouping's Direct | Deep switch comes to this tree too (Deep by default: Stage 1 = 1 246 fixtures, Direct = its 9 folders). PIXSTICK is on the desk a fixture holding 8 fixtures as well — the MVR group and the folder are one idea, drawn one way.
