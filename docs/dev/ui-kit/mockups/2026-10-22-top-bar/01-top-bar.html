The top bar: a status strip with the modes in it

Your three asks: take the idea of A1 S1 (a thin strip of facts across the top), move the left sidebar's Highlight, Solo, Blind, Freeze, Oops and Meow up into it as minimal indicators, and give the green LumaCore dot a better design. This page is the strip and the indicators; page 2 is the mark.

Today, and the idea

Try it

Tap an indicator: the line it runs shows here.

Three layouts

A · the ShowInfo strip

A1 S1 almost as drawn: the mark at the left, a Show tab (gold dot, the show's name, its unsaved dot) that opens the show's info, the facts as Key: Value, then the modes, then the clock. The tab is the strip's “window”: on another screen it can be Command line instead, as in A1 Whole.

B · modes first

The modes right after the mark, where the eye starts; the facts move to the right and the clock ends the strip. Quickest to read a mode, but the facts and the modes sit at the two ends.

C · quiet until something is on

Only the show and the clock always; a mode shows only while it is on, the facts are one tap away on the Show tab. The calmest strip; you can't see at a glance that a mode is off.

The sidebar after the move

The six keys leave the sidebar; Power, Lock, the waiting-attributes key, Menu and At stay. The sidebar gets shorter and quieter, and the modes sit where the eye already checks the show and the time.

What each indicator does

  • Blind, Highlight, Freeze, Solo: lit in their own colour while the mode is on for you (the station's Status.modes). A tap runs Toggle Blind (and so on), the same line as the sidebar key today; hold opens the mode's menu (Highlight: its master).
  • Oops: the number of steps you can undo; a tap runs Oops, hold opens the Oops window. It flashes once when a step is undone.
  • Meow: the paw, gold while latched or the Meow key is held; a tap latches.

Colours are new tokens (--lc-mode-blind uses today's --lc-blind; Highlight, Freeze, Solo get their own), so Daylight and Night restyle them in one place. On a touch screen the strip is 44 px tall and each indicator a 44 px target (the kit's touch rule).

To decide on this page

1. Layout: A (the ShowInfo strip, recommended), B (modes first) or C (quiet until on)?
2. Indicator style: words (always there, lit when on; recommended: you see “off” too), pills (only when on) or dots?
3. Do the six keys leave the sidebar (recommended), or does the sidebar keep them as keys and the strip only shows state?
4. Which facts: Show (and unsaved), User, Profile, DataPool, selected Sequence, LumaSession, the session clock? Anything to add or drop?
5. Should a screen's strip be able to hold the command line instead of the Show tab, as on A1's second screen?
